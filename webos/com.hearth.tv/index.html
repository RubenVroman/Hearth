LG webOS · OLED65G1

Open Hearth

This app only remembers the house address, then opens Hearth fullscreen. Sign in, or paste a machine token, on the next screen. Nothing here is sent to the internet.

LAN default is VAULT at 192.168.2.26 port 8787. Tailscale works only if this TV can route there. Do not put a token in the URL, and do not port-forward Hearth.
